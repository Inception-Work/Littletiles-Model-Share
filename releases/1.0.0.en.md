**LittleTiles Model Share 1.0.0**

- Browse and download public works without signing in. An INC account is required only for account features such as uploading, My Space, favorites and following.
- Added an independent Grid selector: All Grid, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32. Combine it with game version, author and tag filters.
- Mixed-grid collections remain visible when any entry matches. Opening a filtered collection shows only entries with the selected Grid.
- Blueprints are checked against the configured LittleTiles grids and their actual structure data, including child structures, before delivery. Fixed crashes when holding an incompatible blueprint.
- The following feed now covers all followed authors and is paginated by publication time. Bottom controls change feed pages only; recent authors and the full author list have independent navigation.
- Check for mod updates after entering a world. Stable and test builds check their respective channels, with localized, wrapped and scrollable notes plus Download, Later and Never remind actions.
- Improved signed-out labels and public author pages so browsing public content does not force sign-in.
- The companion website now gives newly created mod tokens the default permissions needed to browse, upload, save and download works.
